# Getting Started with Nimbus

## Create a workspace
Sign up with your work email, pick a workspace name, and choose a data region. Setup takes about 5 minutes.

## Connect data sources
Nimbus ships with connectors for PostgreSQL, BigQuery, Snowflake, and Salesforce. Connectors sync every 15 minutes by default, and Pro plans can sync as often as every 5 minutes.

## Invite your team
Administrators can invite teammates by email. Each invited user occupies one paid seat once they accept the invitation.

## Build your first dashboard
Choose a template or start blank, then drag charts onto the canvas. Dashboards can be shared by link or embedded in other tools using an iframe.
