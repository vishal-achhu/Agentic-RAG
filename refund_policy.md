# Nimbus Refund Policy

## Eligibility
Customers on monthly plans can request a full refund within 14 days of their first payment. Annual plans are eligible for a prorated refund within 30 days of purchase. Refunds are not available for usage-based API overage charges.

## How to request
Submit a refund request from Billing settings or email billing@nimbus.example. Requests are reviewed within 3 business days. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method in 5 to 10 business days.

## Cancellation
You may cancel at any time. Cancelling stops future renewals, and access continues until the end of the paid period.
