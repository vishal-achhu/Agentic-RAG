# Nimbus Service Level Agreement

## Uptime commitment
Nimbus guarantees 99.9 percent monthly uptime for Pro plans and 99.99 percent for Enterprise plans. Starter plans have no uptime guarantee.

## Service credits
If monthly uptime falls below the commitment, customers receive service credits. Uptime between 99.0 and 99.9 percent earns a 10 percent credit, and uptime below 99.0 percent earns a 25 percent credit. Credits must be claimed within 30 days of the incident.

## Support response times
Enterprise customers receive a 1 hour first response for critical issues. Pro customers receive a 24 hour response over email. Starter customers use the community forum.

## Maintenance windows
Planned maintenance happens on Sundays between 02:00 and 04:00 UTC and is announced 7 days in advance.
