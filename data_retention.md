# Nimbus Data Retention and Deletion

## Retention periods
Dashboard data is retained for as long as the workspace is active. Raw event logs are kept for 13 months on Pro plans and 24 months on Enterprise plans. Starter plans retain raw event logs for 3 months.

## Deletion
When a workspace is deleted, all customer data is permanently removed within 30 days. Backups are purged within 90 days. Customers can request deletion of individual user records through the privacy portal.

## Data export
Administrators can export all workspace data as CSV or Parquet files at any time. Exports larger than 5 GB are delivered through a secure download link that expires after 7 days.

## Data residency
Customers can choose the United States, European Union, or India region when creating a workspace. The region cannot be changed after creation.
