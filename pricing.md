# Nimbus Pricing and Plans

## Plans
Nimbus offers three subscription plans billed per seat per month.

- Starter plan: $12 per seat per month, includes 5 dashboards and community support.
- Pro plan: $29 per seat per month, includes unlimited dashboards, SSO, and email support with a 24 hour response time.
- Enterprise plan: custom pricing, includes a dedicated success manager, audit logs, and 24/7 phone support.

## Billing
Annual billing gives a 15 percent discount compared with monthly billing. Invoices are issued on the first day of each billing period and are payable within 30 days. Usage above the included API quota is billed at $0.40 per 1,000 extra requests.

## Free trial
New workspaces get a 14 day free trial of the Pro plan. No credit card is required to start the trial.
